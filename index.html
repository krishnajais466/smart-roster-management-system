Conflicts — what needs attention

Mandatory = a required day is uncovered or short-staffed. Preference = coverage is fine, but something is not ideal (e.g. working a public holiday). Every conflict is explained in plain language, with a backup suggestion where one exists. The app never silently generates an invalid roster.

    How to demo this in 3–5 minutes

    1. Click Generate Roster for December 2026. Read the coverage % and the conflict badge at the top.
    2. Switch tabs: Brand-wise → UrbanNest, then Process-wise → CS, then Employee-wise → Kavya Sharma.
    3. Open a conflict card — it explains the gap in plain language and suggests a backup. Click Assign this backup to resolve one.
    4. Click Simulate: sole employee on leave — AuraSkin goes uncovered on 7 Dec. Then click the other simulate button — UrbanNest CS still holds with one person left.
    5. Click Export CSV to download the view you are looking at.
